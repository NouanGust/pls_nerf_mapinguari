## 1. Alamoa (★ Lendária — Conjurador)

> **Adaptação solicitada:** Preservar a cabeleira loira volumosa e etérea, combinada a uma presença cadavérica/esquelética subjacente à beleza fantasmagórica insular.

- **LENDA / ENTIDADE:** Alamoa (A Dama Branca de Fernando de Noronha)
- **CARACTERÍSTICAS QUE DEVEM SER PRESERVADAS:** Longa cabeleira loira ondulante que parece viva ou feita de névoa marinha; silhueta feminina esguia cuja beleza sedutora revela uma estrutura esquelética cadavérica sob a pele translúcida e mortalha de sal e algas; olhar vazio e hipnótico; faíscas de luz fosforescente dourada e fulgor solar ameaçador concentrado nas mãos cadavéricas.
- **FUNÇÃO DA UNIDADE:** Conjurador (frágil à distância, conjura clarão explosivo devastador).
- **ELEMENTO OU PODER PRINCIPAL:** Fulgor Solar e Hipnose Insular (Encanto + Fulgor).
- **ESTÁGIO DE EVOLUÇÃO:** Avançado (Lendária ★).

### Prompt em Inglês (Prompt for Image Generation / Concept Art):

```text
A dark Brazilian folklore concept art board of the Alamoa legend for a dark fantasy auto-chess game. 
Single full-body character portrait centered in three-quarter elevated view on an aged forbidden manuscript parchment. 
Visual style: ancient bestiary etching, woodcut and fine metal engraving, dense ink cross-hatching, deep black ink washes, subtle sepia and soiled ivory parchment texture, dramatic chiaroscuro with a single discrete accent color of luminous pale amber-gold glow. 

Subject: The Alamoa, the spectral siren ghost of Fernando de Noronha archipelago. A tall, ethereal female apparition whose terrifying nature emerges from her dual anatomy: mesmerizing, cascading pale blonde hair floating unnaturally like sea mist and ocean foam, framing a hauntingly gaunt face where delicate pale features fade into a translucent, desiccated skeletal skull visible beneath paper-thin skin. Bony, elongated claw-like fingers held in a delicate summoning gesture, gathering a crackling sphere of blinding solar embers and radiant golden light between her palms. She is draped in tattered, salt-encrusted nocturnal shrouds clinging like decomposing kelp and dried seafoam around her ribcage and skeletal pelvis. Bare bony feet hovering barely inches above volcanic island stone and sea spray. 

Auto-chess unit readability: crisp, unmistakable silhouette for high-angle tactical view; clear distinction between the radiant blonde hair crown, slender skeletal torso, and the floating light source. No action-obscuring pose, no background clutter, no text, no letters, no borders, no ornate frames, no UI. Ominous, ancient, seductive yet terrifying folk horror.
```
